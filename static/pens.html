Every G2 pen donated to the Republic is counted into the national vault and paid for out of the Treasury. The Reserve is the hard asset behind the Cybuck — which is why it pays far more than a pen costs.

Why the Republic buys pens

The Cybuck was floated on nothing but goodwill. Every note in circulation was backed by the promise that other citizens would keep accepting it — which works right up until the day it doesn't. The Republic resolved that a currency ought to stand on something you can hold.

Gold was impractical. What Cyvathon chose instead was the one object every citizen already carries: the G2. It is also constitutionally necessary — a decree is only law once it has been signed into the Gazette in G2 ink, so a Republic without a working stock of pens is a Republic that cannot legislate at all.

So the Reserve pays 400 CB, far above what a pen is worth on any shelf. It isn't buying stationery. It's buying backing: a pen in the national vault is worth more to Cyvathon than the same pen in a pencil case. Reserve holdings count toward national GDP for exactly that reason.

Donate to the Reserve

Every donated pen goes to the Registrar, who holds the national vault. Nothing is paid until they have the pens in front of them and count them in — a reserve is only a reserve if the asset is actually in the vault. Cyvazon will carry them if you'd rather not go and find them.
