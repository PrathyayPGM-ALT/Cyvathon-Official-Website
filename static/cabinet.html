Cabinet Powers

Ministers govern their own brief. Some things are duties — delegated outright, carried out without asking, because making the President countersign every logged pen would only move the bottleneck. Everything that moves a national lever is proposed, not imposed: the minister asks, and nothing changes until the President assents.

Every brief in the Cabinet and what it carries. A ministry picks up a brief from its name — call one "Ministry of Defence" and whoever holds it works the Armoury.
